# Words

- **Quote**: a request for work and the offer made for it; the root of a
  chain. One request is one quote, however many suppliers are asked.
- **Booking**: accepted work being arranged. A quote may lead to several.
- **Job**: one piece of work carried out. It may sit under a booking or
  stand alone.
- **Chain**: a quote with its bookings and their jobs, or a job that stands
  alone. It follows one SOP.
- **SOP**: the business's written process, published as versions. Each
  version lists phases and milestones for the quote, booking and job
  stages.
- **Phase**: a named group of milestones on one work item. It has no status
  of its own and implies no order.
- **Milestone**: an outcome that evidence can show, such as "Deposit
  received".
- **Judgment**: one conclusion about one milestone, in one of four words:
  done, pending, blocked, failed. It cites evidence.
- **Memo**: the note left by each review: what changed, what was decided,
  what is waiting.
- **Evidence**: an update recorded unchanged, with where it came from and
  when; files are stored by the hash of their bytes.
- **Party**: a person, organization or agent. A **role** says what a party
  is on a work item, or to another party.
- **The person**: whoever answers the agent's questions. **The operator**:
  the business that runs the ledger.
