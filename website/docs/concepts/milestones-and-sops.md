# Milestones and SOPs

A **milestone** is one step you track, written as an outcome an email can
show: "Deposit received", not "Chase the deposit". Each quote, booking and
job has its own milestones. A **phase** is an optional named group of them.

An **SOP** (standard operating procedure) is your checklist, written once:
the phases and milestones for the quote, booking and job stages. Each
milestone has a **criterion** that says what evidence shows it is met, in
your own words:

> Cited evidence shows the customer accepting the price in writing. A
> verbal yes reported by staff is not enough.

The ledger never evaluates a criterion. The agent reads it when it judges.

## Versions

An SOP is published as a version, such as v1. A published version never
changes. To change your process, publish v2.

Attaching a version to work is called **adopting** it. The agent adopts a
version at the quote, the one you chose or set as the default, and the quote's bookings and jobs follow it with no
further step. A job with no quote adopts its own.

Work keeps the version it started on. When you retire a version, no new
work can adopt it, but work already on it stays on it.

## Work without an SOP

The ledger allows it, but the skills never judge a milestone on work that
follows no SOP. Until an SOP is adopted, the agent only leaves a memo saying
what arrived. Write your SOP first: see [Write your SOP](../guides/write-your-sop.md)
and the [example SOPs](../examples.md).
