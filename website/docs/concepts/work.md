# Quotes, bookings and jobs

Every piece of work moves through up to three stages.

```
 quote ──┬── booking ──┬── job        the offer, the customer's yes,
         │             └── job        and the work itself
         └── booking ───── job
```

- **Quote.** A request for work and the offer made for it.
- **Booking.** The customer said yes. A booking sits under one quote, and a
  quote can have several.
- **Job.** One piece of work being carried out: a site visit, a shipment, a
  maintenance appointment. A job can sit under one booking, or stand on its
  own.

A quote with its bookings and their jobs is one **chain**. A job with no
booking is a chain of one.

## Why all three stages

Many businesses track only the job: the shipment, the install. But the
quote stage shows which offers are waiting on the customer, and the booking
stage holds what must be agreed before work starts: the signature, the
deposit, the date. Skip them and the work you are still winning, or have
won but not arranged, is invisible. We recommend all three.

## Outside reference numbers

Other systems' numbers for the same work, such as an order number or an
invoice number, are recorded as outside references. A later email that
quotes one can then be traced to its work. Matching ignores case and
punctuation, so `REF-10422` and `ref 10422` match.
