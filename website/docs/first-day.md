# Your first day

There is no sample data. You try the ledger on your own work, which is the
only test that tells you anything.

## 1. Connect your inbox

Make sure your agent can reach your email, through your mail tool. See
[Connect your mail](guides/connect-your-mail.md). If you have no mail tool
yet, you can paste emails into the chat.

## 2. Write your SOP

Learn what an SOP and a milestone are, then write your SOP with the agent.
See [Milestones and SOPs](concepts/milestones-and-sops.md) and
[Write your SOP](guides/write-your-sop.md). The agent will not mark
progress on work that has no SOP.

## 3. Record one real job

Pick one real quote, booking or job. Have the agent find every past email
about it, oldest first, and record them. Then ask:

> What is waiting on me?

The agent answers with the questions only you can answer and the
follow-ups that are due, each with the email behind it.
